**Supplementary Table S2. Sensitivity of the threshold to the two arbitrary conventions of the criterion.**

ELDERMET cohort, *n*<sub>sp</sub> = 5, *H*(*hdcA*) = 0.1522. The fidelity cut-off is the ρ that a family must reach; the retention level is the probability with which it must do so. Each cell is the median of the eight seeds, as everywhere in the paper; the bracketed interval is the range over those seeds. `hdcA` × is *H*(*hdcA*) divided by that cell's median threshold.

| 0.85 | 0.1087 (0.0644–0.1204) ×1.4 | 0.0446 (0.0386–0.0651) ×3.4 | 0.0218 (0.0121–0.0307) ×7.0 |
| 0.90 | 0.0594 (0.0504–0.0800) ×2.6 | 0.0378 (0.0340–0.0506) ×4.0 | 0.0201 (0.0104–0.0278) ×7.6 |
| 0.95 | 0.0397 (0.0358–0.0477) ×3.8 | 0.0254 (0.0204–0.0351) ×6.0 | 0.0116 (0.0070–0.0229) ×13.2 |

Across the nine cells the median threshold spans 0.0116–0.1087, a 9.4-fold range, and the *hdcA* margin runs ×1.4 to ×13.2 — it exceeds unity in all nine. Taking, in each cell, the least favourable of the eight seeds instead of the median, the smallest margin in the grid is ×1.3, so no choice of convention reverses the verdict; only the margin moves.

The permissive corner (cut-off 0.95, retention 99%) has both the lowest threshold and the widest seed range (0.0070–0.0229, 3.3-fold), which is why the grid under the median convention (0.0116–0.1087) is wider than the single-draw version first reported (0.021–0.117, 1.3–7.1-fold).
